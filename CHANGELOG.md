### Changed

-   Unified all module, nominal type, enum-variant, primitive associated-constant (`u32.MAX`, `f64.PI`, `f64.TAU`), and `use`-tree (`use cx.fs.{self, File};`) paths onto `.` (`ADR-21A`), featuring `prev_sig_kind` nested tuple-field scanning (`tup.0.1.2`), bounded `LL(k)` `isTypeStart` / `isStaticPathExpr` disambiguation, elevated `|>` pipeline precedence `(10, 11)` above `orelse` `(9, 8)` (`ADR-21C`), updated `corex fmt` `DocArena` layout rules, and full `DR-21` corpus migration across Stage-1 `core/**/*.cx`, benchmarks, snapshots, drift guards, and 4-tier differential/fuzz suites.
-   Canonicalized `corex fmt` normalization (`Option[T]` to `T?`, `Result[T, E]` to `T!E`, redundant `-> unit` stripping, lexicographic single-line marker `@attr` sorting, `42_u64` suffix `_` insertion, `extern "C"` casing, and delta-preserving `"""…"""` multiline string indentation) and migrated all 12 Stage-1 `core/**/*.cx` kernel modules plus the entire test/benchmark/snapshot corpus to canonical CoreX v1.0 syntax (`DR-22`).
-   Expanded `TargetSpec` (`4B`, repurposing `_pad` as `abi: TargetAbi` with `CfgEnv` parity) from 7 to all 11 canonical cross-compilation targets (`x86_64-linux-{gnu,musl}`, `aarch64-linux-{gnu,musl}`, `{x86_64,aarch64}-apple-darwin`, `x86_64-windows-{msvc,gnu}`, `aarch64-windows-msvc`, `wasm32-unknown-{wasi,freestanding}`), widened implicit `Context` from 8 slots (`64B`) to 10 slots (`80B` on 64-bit / `40B` on `wasm32`, adding `reactor` and `reactor_ctx`), and sorted `CLI_SUBCOMMANDS` (`bench`, `build`, `check`, `fmt`, `lsp`, `run`, `test`, `translate-c`, `upgrade`) alphabetically across `router.zig`, `cli_help.snap`, and drift guards.

### Removed

-   Retired the legacy `::` path separator (`E0112` with machine-applicable `.` replacement), `if let` and `while let` constructs (`E0216` with `is` / `:=` replacement), built-in `std` root namespace, `Option` `catch` handling, and built-in `Option.unwrap` / `Option.unwrap_or` methods in favor of unified `.` paths, `is` / `:=` condition bindings, `cx.*`, and `orelse`.
-   Retired `&&` logical AND (`E0113`), `\{` / `\}` and lone `}` format braces (`E0114`), `||` logical OR (`E0219`), prefix `!x` logical NOT (`E0220`), `let mut` / `var mut` / `mut ident` / `ref` / `inout` / `sink` / `set` / `own x: T` / `mut self` (`E0221`), `@attr(key = val)` (`E0223`), `;` inside struct/`bit_field`/`enum` member lists (`E0224`), `Numeric` and `@noalloc` (`E0225`), `*const T` (`E0226`), prefix `*ptr` dereference (`E0227`), and `.len` / `.ptr` / `.is_empty` slice/array fields (`E0424`), each with deterministic `SuggestedFix` auto-rewrites.
